Panda Remit Korea First-Time User Guide

A step-by-step guide for overseas users sending money from South Korea with Panda Remit for the first time: download, registration, KYC, Korean bank account linking, order creation, payment, and tracking.

Before you start: Use your own Korean mobile number, valid ID information, and Korean bank account. Exchange rates, fees, available destinations, and delivery times may change depending on the transfer corridor, banking systems, and compliance review. Always follow the latest information shown in the app when placing an order.
Step 01 · Get Started

Download the Panda Remit app

Search for “Panda Remit” on the App Store or Google Play, then download the app for free.

  • Use a phone that can receive SMS verification codes from a Korean mobile number.
Step 02 · Registration

Sign up with a Korean mobile number

Open the app, register with your Korean mobile number, and set your login password.

  • Make sure the number is active and avoid reusing passwords from other platforms.
Step 03 · Identity Check

Complete KYC verification

Follow the in-app instructions to upload your Korean ID information and personal details. Panda Remit works with GLN to support secure verification and compliance checks.

  • Use your own valid information. Keep ID photos clear, complete, and unobstructed.
Step 04 · Profile Details

Review your personal information

Continue following the on-screen instructions and carefully check your name, ID details, and contact information before submitting.

  • Incorrect or inconsistent information may cause verification failure or transfer delays.
Step 05 · Payment Source

Link your Korean bank account

Connect your own Korean bank account and complete the 1 KRW verification. This account will be used as your payment source.

  • The source deck states that no fee applies for this verification. Always follow the latest information shown in the app.
Step 06 · Final Setup

Set your payment password and address

Set your payment password and complete address verification so your account is ready for payment authorization.

  • Keep your payment password private and never share it with anyone.
Step 07 · Rates and Fees

Check exchange rates, fees, and destinations

Choose the destination country or region and enter the transfer amount to review the exchange rate, receiving amount, and fee. Supported destinations include Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, Japan, the USA, Europe, and more.

  • Rates, fees, and available destinations may change. The actual details shown in the app at the time of order should be treated as final.
Step 08 · Create Order

Enter recipient information

Fill in the recipient’s name, bank account, and other required details, then confirm everything before creating the transfer order.

  • Use the name format required by the receiving bank. Incorrect account details may cause rejection or delays.
Step 09 · Payment

Pay from your linked bank account

Complete the payment using your linked Korean bank account. After payment, you can check the order status and confirmation details in the app.

  • The source deck mentions that transfers usually arrive in 3–5 minutes. Actual delivery time depends on the corridor, bank systems, and risk review.
Step 10 · Completion

Track processing and completion

Once payment is confirmed, processing starts. Most transfers can complete quickly, but the final status should always be checked in the app.

  • If a transfer takes longer than expected, check the order status first and then contact Panda Remit support if needed.
